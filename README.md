# The Ultra Sprint

A condensed, AI-assisted method for going from a raw idea to a tested prototype in less than 2 days.

---

## What is this?

The Ultra Sprint is an open-source method inspired by the [Google Ventures Design Sprint](https://www.gv.com/sprint/). It compresses the classic five-day sprint into a single **sprint day (~6 hours)** plus a separate **test day (~2.5 hours)**, by leaning on AI tools for research, challenge generation, and prototyping.

It is designed for small teams of 2–5 participants and a dedicated facilitator.

## How is it structured?

The sprint day has four parts:

| Part | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Frame** | 1 hour | Define challenges, a dream goal, and the biggest obstacles |
| **2. Research & Sketch** | 1.5 hours | Find inspiration, then sketch individual solutions |
| **3. Present & Decide** | 30 min | Share concepts, give feedback, vote on the one to prototype |
| **4. Prototype** | 2.5 hours | Build a testable prototype using AI tools |

On test day, 5 potential users each spend 25 minutes interacting with the prototype and sharing their reactions.

## Who is this for?

Innovation teams, startup teams, freelancers, hackathon participants, student projects - anyone who needs to validate an idea quickly with real users.
